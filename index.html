Cytonaut

You are a speck adrift inside a living human beta cell, the cell that makes insulin. Fly anywhere. Point at anything to learn what it is and what it does. Every machine you see is running.

Building the cell…

This experience needs a keyboard and mouse. On a phone or tablet you can watch the cell, but not fly through it.

About accuracy. Organelles are drawn at their true relative sizes. Molecular machines are enlarged, usually 2 to 8 times, and their numbers reduced, so you can see them work; every process is slowed, and each entry says by how much. You can pass through membranes, which a real particle could not. Each entry lists what was simplified.
